Updated Model (v2.0)

1. High-entropy intensity: 0.51 kg CO₂ / $ – derived from EXIOBASE 3.8+ (top 20 % most carbon-intensive final-demand sectors). Replaces prior 0.409 g/$.

2. Displacement rate δ: 34% (95 % CI 24%–48%) – weighted average from 2023-2025 investor surveys (Coinbase, Motley Fool, Chainalysis). Retail: ~33 %, HNW: ~59 %, Institutions: ~8 %.

Equation: Net = Σ(ΔCap × δ × I) − Mining

Note: We are taking a conservative approach and considering that outflows also displaces back to high-entropy intensity sectors

Break-even Displacement Rate (δ)

Question: At what % of BTC $inflows must come from high-entropy spending for Bitcoin to break even on CO₂?

    δ_break = Mining (Mt/day) / (Δcap ($/day) × 0.51 kg CO₂/$)
  • Daily mining (Mt CO2): ~0.15 Mt / day in 2025 (Baseline), 0.10 Mt (Renewables 2030)
  • Daily inflow: $1.8B (2025 avg)
  • Intensity: 0.51 kg CO₂ per $ (EXIOBASE)

Result:

  • Baseline: δ = 16.3% → Bitcoin breaks even
  • Renewables 2030: δ = 10.9% → only 11% needed
  • Current δ = 34% → 2.1× safety margin

Even in a clean-grid future, only 1 in 9 dollars needs to come from luxury/real-estate for Bitcoin to be net CO₂ positive.
